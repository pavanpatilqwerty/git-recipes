Mirchi Bhajji Recipe

Description

Mirchi Bhajji, also known as Mirchi Bajji or Mirapakaya Bajji, is a popular Indian street food, especially in South India. It is made using large green chillies that are coated in a spiced gram flour batter and deep-fried until golden and crispy.

The outer layer is crunchy, while the inside is soft, spicy, and flavourful. It is commonly served hot with coconut chutney, tomato sauce, or mint chutney. Mirchi Bhajji is a popular evening snack, especially during the monsoon season, and is best enjoyed with a cup of hot tea.

Ingredients

  • 6 large green chillies (preferably bajji chillies)
  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 2 tablespoons rice flour
  • half a teaspoon red chilli powder
  • one-fourth a teaspoon turmeric powder
  • half a teaspoon cumin seeds
  • A pinch of baking soda (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as required
  • Oil for deep frying
  • ½ teaspoon ajwain (carom seeds)

Steps

  1. Wash the green chillies thoroughly and pat them dry with a clean kitchen towel.
  2. Make a lengthwise slit in each chilli without cutting it completely. Remove the seeds if you prefer a less spicy bhajji.
  3. In a mixing bowl, add gram flour, rice flour, red chilli powder, turmeric powder, cumin seeds, ajwain, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Gradually add water and mix everything until you get a smooth, thick batter. Make sure there are no lumps.
  5. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat.
  6. Dip each green chilli into the prepared batter, making sure it is completely coated.
  7. Carefully drop the coated chillies into the hot oil.
  8. Deep-fry them on medium heat, turning occasionally, until they become golden brown and crispy.
  9. Remove the bhajjis from the oil and place them on a paper towel to absorb excess oil.
  10. Serve hot with coconut chutney, mint chutney, or tomato ketchup.
